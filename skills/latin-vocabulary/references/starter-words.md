# Starter vocabulary

Status: small original exercise set, awaiting independent teaching review. This is not the App's remote question bank and not a vocabulary-size test. Long-vowel marks are omitted consistently in these headwords; do not infer that the words have no long vowels.

| ID | Dictionary form | Gender | Core sense for the starter exercise | Common acceptable answer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| LV001 | aqua, aquae | feminine | 水 | water |
| LV002 | rosa, rosae | feminine | 玫瑰 | rose / 玫瑰花 |
| LV003 | via, viae | feminine | 道路 | road / way / 路 |
| LV004 | porta, portae | feminine | 门；城门 | gate / gateway |
| LV005 | terra, terrae | feminine | 土地；大地 | earth / land / ground |
| LV006 | luna, lunae | feminine | 月亮 | moon |
| LV007 | liber, libri | masculine | 书 | book / 书籍 |
| LV008 | agricola, agricolae | masculine | 农夫 | farmer / 农民 |

These senses are appropriate for a small beginner set, not exhaustive dictionary entries. If a learner gives another possible sense, consider context and explain it instead of blindly rejecting it.

For LV007, explicitly ask about the noun `liber, libri`. Bare unmarked `liber` can also be associated with the adjective `liber, libera, liberum` (free); an ambiguous question must not penalize a learner for noticing this.

Constructed examples for explanations:

- `Librum lego.` — I read/am reading a book.
- `Luna lucet.` — The moon shines.
- `Agricola rosam amat.` — The farmer loves a rose.

Use examples after recall or when the learner asks for help. The goal is understanding, not exposing the answer before every question.
